Destination guide

Where would you like to surf?

Lapoint groups lessons by experience. What you want to do once you’re back on shore can help you narrow down these three camps.

Portugal · Atlantic

Ericeira

From the cliff above Ribeira d’Ilhas you can watch the swell lines form before they reach the beach. Ericeira, a fishing village on Portugal’s west coast, is home to Europe’s first World Surfing Reserve.

Lapoint runs lessons for levels 1 to 4 here. After a session, follow the cobbled streets into town for a plate of local fish.

Explore the Ericeira camp

Costa Rica · Pacific

Santa Teresa

The forest meets the sand in Santa Teresa. From the Lapoint house, it’s about a five-minute walk to one of the breaks where the instructors teach.

The camp teaches levels 1 to 3. Lapoint recommends the December to March dry season for smaller waves. On a day off, head to Montezuma for its waterfalls.

Explore the Santa Teresa camp

Sri Lanka · Indian Ocean

Ahangama

On Sri Lanka’s south coast, the local team travels between surf spots by tuk-tuk. The water is warm enough to leave your wetsuit at home.

The camp teaches levels 1 to 4. Between sessions, set aside time for Mirissa’s beach or wander through the lanes of Galle Fort.

Choosing your camp

Before picking a destination, picture a wave you’d like to be able to ride by the end of the trip.

How do I choose my level?

Tell the team whether you need help standing up, can catch unbroken waves on your own or want to work on turns. That gives them a starting point for finding the right group.

What if I’m travelling solo?

Ask about rooms and when guests get together for meals and surf sessions. You can then plan time with the group and time to explore by yourself.

What should I check in the package?

Check which lessons, equipment and meals are included for your dates. Ask how to reach the camp from the airport and which outings you’ll need to arrange separately.
